## What you may do

- Use the name to refer to the project truthfully: «based on Neruna», «a fork of Neruna», «compatible with Neruna».
- Use the unmodified official builds and point to [neruna.org](https://neruna.org).
- Write about Neruna in articles, reviews, tutorials and talks.

## What you may not do without written permission

- Distribute a modified version (a fork) under the name «Neruna» or a name containing it, for example
  «Neruna Professional Edition», «Neruna Plus» or «MyCompany Neruna».
- Use the Neruna logo or application icon for a modified version, a product or a service.
- Suggest that your product, service or company is official, endorsed by or affiliated with Neruna.
- Register domains, social media accounts or company names containing «Neruna» for a related offer.

A fork must use its own name and its own logo, so that nobody confuses it with the original.
